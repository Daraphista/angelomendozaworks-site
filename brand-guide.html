01 Logo

One paddle, two letters.

The coral shaft cuts across the A and M like a crossbar. It reads as direction and as a clear view through the work.

Primary · on Cloud
On Lagoon
Reversed · on Ink
On Cobalt
On Sand
One colour · print, stamps
Clear space

Keep one paddle-blade width (the height of the blade, about ⅙ of the mark) free on every side.

Minimum size
Web · 75px
Nav · 40px
Min · 24px

Below 24px use the favicon version: mark on a Lagoon rounded square with a heavier stroke.

Don’t
  • Recolor the paddle anything but Coral (or Ink in one-colour use).
  • Rotate the paddle to another angle, or move it off the letters.
  • Add shadows, outlines or gradients.
  • Place the mark on busy photo areas. Use a calm patch of sky or water, or the reversed version.

04 Imagery

Real moments, high sun.

Photos should feel like someone was actually there.

  • Wide angle, close subject, horizon visible.
  • Saturated sky and water, daylight only.
  • Add subtle halftone grain on large placements.
  • One coral object per frame when you can.
Avoid
  • Stock handshakes, laptops on desks, glowing network graphics.
  • Palm trees, sunsets and other resort clichés.
  • Heavy filters that shift the water away from turquoise.
05 Voice

Short, warm, first person.

Talk like a friend who happens to know ads and code.

Enterprise-grade performance optimization at scale.
Say it plainly

Numbers and verbs over adjectives.

Stay human

“I” and “you”. Contractions are fine.

Keep the fun light

One playful line per page. The work stays serious.

06 Interface

Round, soft, buoyant.

Rules for the site refresh that follows this kit.

Buttons

Pill shape. One Coral button per screen. Coral buttons carry Ink text.

Shape
  • Cards 16px radius, buttons and chips fully round.
  • Soft shadow, never a hard border plus shadow.
  • Lagoon and Sand for large fills, Cloud for the page.
Motion
  • Gentle lift (−2px) on hover, 150ms.
  • Section dividers use the wave line.
  • Respect reduced motion.
